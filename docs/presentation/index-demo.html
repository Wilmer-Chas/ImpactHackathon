01 The Case

Oversight without an evidence trail is a risk.

A TM product owner must brief the Management Oversight Committee — with scattered signals and little time.

  • Evidence lives across changes, incidents, risk, performance, and releases — not in one brief.
  • Upper org needs a clear stance: Authorize / with conditions / Hold.
  • Concrete tension: Change #1001 on TM-Core while open severity and elevated backlog remain.

02 The Promise

What we will show you.

01

02

A monthly Fraud Detection deck you can export to PPT Numbers from SQLite. Narrative from the model.

03

An enterprise Risk Report with approve / reject Flags issues still open at period end — for a timeframe you pick.

03 The Proof · 1/3

Live: assistant with real citations.

On Home, ask about August performance and open risks. Point at the evidence IDs — they resolve to seeded rows, not freeform guesses.

Switch to the app → Employee Home → Assistant sidebar

Suggested prompt: “What drove August performance, and which risks are still open?”

03 The Proof · 2/3

Live: Fraud Report → PPT.

Open the monthly Fraud Detection deck (Trend Watch is a fast entry). Show the anomaly on the chart, then export the compliance pack.

App → Trend Watch or Fraud Report → Export PPT

Call out: chart numbers from SQLite; narrative from the model.

03 The Proof · 3/3

Live: Risk Report — open at period end.

Set timeframe Jun–Sep 2026. Show issues still open at period end, then approve or reject a category with mitigation.

App → Risk Report → set from/to → approve / reject → optional PPT

Optional deeper beat: MOC → Change #1001 decision brief if time allows.

04 The Insight

AI that briefs — without inventing the evidence.

Bound to the pack

Citations and numeric scores resolve to real rows. The model narrates; the database owns the facts.

Fail closed

If the model is down or structured output breaks, narrative APIs fail — we do not fabricate oversight copy.

Humans decide

Impact recommends. The product owner and MOC authorize, condition, or hold — accountability stays with people.
